# Takyon name and logo policy

The code is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`). That
licence covers copyright only. It says so itself: "Patent and trademark rights
are not licensed under this Public License." This page covers the name and the
logo.

"Takyon™" and the Takyon mark identify the official builds published from
<https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/Takyon>. The name is not a registered trademark
yet. It is used here as an unregistered mark, which is why it carries ™ and
never ®.

## What the mark is

- The name **Takyon**.
- The logo: the path data in `brand/geometry.js` and `docs/brand.md`, and every
  icon generated from it under `brand/svg/`, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/` and
  `apps/desktop/public/`.

These assets are all rights reserved. The licence does not cover them.

## What you may do

- Say your project is "based on Takyon" or "a fork of Takyon" in its
  description, README or about screen. The licence requires that credit anyway.
- Refer to Takyon by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and talks.
- Link to the official repository.
- Run, study and modify Takyon with the logo in place for your own
  noncommercial use, including builds you never distribute.

## What you may not do

- Distribute a modified version under the name Takyon, or a confusingly similar
  name such as "Takyon Plus" or "Tachyon Launcher".
- Distribute a modified version with the Takyon logo or icons. Replace them
  with your own before sharing it.
- Suggest that V3SPER made, endorses or supports your version.
- Use the name or logo in a product name, domain name, app store listing or
  social media handle.
