# v0.18 Date arithmetic

The calculator Source answers explicit calendar questions offline. This phase was requested on 2026-10-28. It extends the existing Calc card and uses `jiff`, already present for time zones.

## Scope and rules

| Question | Answer rule |
|---|---|
| `30 days from now is what date` | Add 30 civil days to local today; show full date and weekday. |
| `how many days is 20th September 2027 in` | Target date minus local today. Today is zero; tomorrow is one; past dates are negative. |
| `days since 20 Sep 2027` | Local today minus target date. |
| `1 month from 31 jan 2027` | Clamp to final day of target month. A leap-year shift follows calendar rules. |
| `days until 25 dec` | Choose next occurrence on or after today. `since` chooses previous occurrence on or before today. |
| `what day of the week is 20 Sep 2027?` | Show weekday as copied answer, with full date on card. |
| `days between 21 Dec 2027 and 5 Apr 2039` | Show signed second date minus first date, in days. Both endpoints appear on card. |
| `next Friday` | Choose first Friday strictly after local today. |
| `two weeks from Friday` | Add 14 days to next Friday, using same strict rule. |
| `how many weeks until September 10` | Show whole weeks plus leftover days; signed for past dates. |
| `last Friday`, `this Friday`, `Friday in 3 weeks` | Last is strictly before today; this uses the current Monday–Sunday week; the last form adds three weeks to the next Friday. |
| `day after tomorrow`, `September 10 next year` | Resolve against the local calendar date. |
| `days left in this month`, `end of next month` | Count after today, or show the period's final date. |
| `3 months and 10 days from now` | Apply up to four calendar units in spoken order, clamping month and year steps. Long expressions wrap on the Calc card. |
| `days between September 10 and October 2` | Resolve the first date to its next occurrence, then the second on or after the first. |
| `10 business days from now` | Count Monday–Friday; exclude the start, include the target. Holidays count as weekdays. |
| `is 2028 a leap year?`, `what's the first Monday in November 2027?` | Answer calendar facts from civil dates. A yearless fifth weekday searches future years within one 400-year Gregorian cycle. |
| `how old will I be on 10 Sep 2027 if born 20 Sep 2000` | Require the birth date in the query. No personal date is stored. |

Accepted anchors are today, now, tomorrow, yesterday, and an explicit date. Accepted shifts use days, weeks, months, and years. Dates accept day-first month names, month-first month names, and ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. A two-digit year or locale-dependent numeric date is refused. The Calc card copies its right-hand answer on Enter.

Past-tense date questions choose the last occurrence of a date without a year. Yearless spans resolve both dates in order and show their years on the card. A 29 February birthday waits for the next real leap day; age increments on 1 March in non-leap years.

Only complete question forms claim a Bangless query. Bare dates, `today`, and partial phrases remain searches. `Policy::Explicit` still requires `=`. Unit conversion retains priority over date parsing. No network request occurs.

## Other useful questions and limits

The grammar also covers `30 days ago`, `45 days ago was?`, `what date was 45 days ago?`, `what's the date 45 days from now?`, `in 45 days, what date will it be?`, `2 weeks before today`, `today plus 3 days`, `which day falls 4123 days from 21 dec 2027`, `what weekday was 45 days ago?`, `what date is tomorrow`, `how long until 20 Sep 2027?`, and `days since 1 Jan 2026`. Number words two through ten work in shifts. It does not yet handle holidays, inclusive counting, localized month names, larger number words, or birthdays without a date in the query. Those need separate grammar and policy decisions; a wrong Calc Entry takes top rank.

Business days mean weekdays only. A future calendar connection may add user-specific holidays and events, but must make the calendar source and region visible with the answer. The current Bangless path remains offline.
