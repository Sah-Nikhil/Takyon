# v0.18 Date arithmetic

The calculator Source answers explicit calendar questions offline. This phase was requested on 2026-10-28. It extends the existing Calc card and uses `jiff`, already present for time zones.

## Scope and rules

| Question | Answer rule |
|---|---|
| `30 days from now is what date` | Add 30 civil days to local today; show full date and weekday. |
| `how many days is 20th September 2027 in` | Target date minus local today. Today is zero; tomorrow is one; past dates are negative. |
| `days since 20 Sep 2027` | Local today minus target date. |
| `1 month from 31 jan 2027` | Clamp to final day of target month. A leap-year shift follows calendar rules. |
| `days until 25 dec` | Choose next occurrence on or after today. `since` chooses previous occurrence on or before today. |

Accepted anchors are today, now, tomorrow, yesterday, and an explicit date. Accepted shifts use days, weeks, months, and years. Dates accept day-first month names, month-first month names, and ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. A two-digit year or locale-dependent numeric date is refused. The Calc card copies its right-hand answer on Enter.

Only complete question forms claim a Bangless query. Bare dates, `today`, and partial phrases remain searches. `Policy::Explicit` still requires `=`. Unit conversion retains priority over date parsing. No network request occurs.

## Other useful questions and limits

The grammar also covers `30 days ago`, `2 weeks before today`, `today plus 3 days`, `what date is tomorrow`, `how many days away is 20 Sep 2027`, and `days since 1 Jan 2026`. It does not yet handle `next Friday`, `business days`, holidays, inclusive counting, `day after tomorrow`, localized month names, number words beyond one, or age calculations. Those need separate grammar and policy decisions; a wrong Calc Entry takes top rank.
