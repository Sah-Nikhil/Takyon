# v0.18 Date arithmetic manual verification

Run against a build made with `bun run build`. Record OS date, time zone, calculator Policy, result, and build version.

1. With Automatic Policy, enter `30 days from now is what date`. Compare the full date and weekday with a trusted calendar. Press Enter and confirm the copied text equals the answer.
2. Enter `how many days is 20th September 2027 in`. Count calendar days from local today to target, excluding today. Confirm the card's target date and copied day count.
3. Check `days until tomorrow`, `days until yesterday`, and `days since yesterday`. Expect `1 day`, `-1 day`, and `1 day`.
4. Check `1 month from 31 jan 2028` and `1 year from 29 feb 2028`. Expect `29 February 2028` and `28 February 2029`.
5. Enter `today`, `20 september 2027`, `7 days to die`, and `days until dawn`. Confirm no Calc card displaces search Entries.
6. Switch to Explicit Policy. Confirm `30 days from now` has no Calc card and `=30 days from now` does.
7. Confirm `2 days to hours` still answers `48 h`, and `time in tokyo` still gives a zone card.
8. Run `bun run bench --alt-hotkey` and record all four budgets, especially first Entry under 30 ms.
9. Check `what day of the week is 20 Sep 2027?`, `days between 21 Dec 2027 and 5 Apr 2039`, and their copied answers. Expect `Monday` and `4123 days`.
10. Check `next Friday` and `two weeks from Friday`. On a Friday, expect the first answer seven days ahead and the second 21 days ahead. Confirm a bare `Friday` remains a search.
11. Check `weeks until 10 September`, `days between 10 September and 2 October`, and `days left in this month`. Confirm displayed years and day-count conventions.
12. Check `last Friday`, `this Friday`, `Friday in 3 weeks`, `day after tomorrow`, and `10 September next year` against a calendar. Check the same queries after local midnight.
13. Check `3 months and 10 days from now` and `1 year, 2 months ago` at month ends. Confirm units apply in typed order.
14. Check `10 business days from now` and `how many weekdays until 10 September`. Count Monday–Friday only; a holiday on a weekday still counts.
15. Check `is 2028 a leap year?`, `what's the first Monday in November 2027?`, and an age query with an explicit birth date. Confirm `days until my birthday` alone remains a search.
16. In Compact mode, enter `1 month and 1 day and 1 month and 1 day from now`. Confirm every step remains visible on the card. Check `what's the fifth Monday in February` against a calendar.
