`%LOCALAPPDATA%\v3sper\takyon\clips\`. Its `.bin` names are random 32-hex
identifiers, not row ids. Record filenames before and after deletion rather than
assuming an id maps to a filename. Prepare four distinguishable image clips on
the previous day: three for section C and one for retention in section D. If
they are unavailable, prepare them and resume those sections tomorrow. Keep
retention longer than one day until section D.
